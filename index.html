How it works

  1. 1

    Describe

    Write events the way you would say them: "Vacation August 1 to 15", "Review on the last Friday of every month at 4pm".

  2. 2

    Review

    Every result appears as a card you can check, edit or remove before anything is added to your calendar.

  3. 3

    Export

    Download a standard .ics file and import it into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

Everything a real calendar needs

  • Recurring events

    Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly: "first Monday", "last day of the month", "every two weeks", until a date or a number of times.

  • Change events with AI

    "Move the gym to 8pm" or "cancel next Wednesday's gym". You see a before and after preview to apply or discard.

  • Time zones

    "Call with the Tokyo office Thursday 9am Tokyo time" lands at the right local time, and repeats keep their time across daylight saving.

  • Reminders

    Reminders are exported as alarms, so your calendar app notifies you.

  • Clash warnings

    You are told when a new or changed event overlaps another timed event.

  • Day, week, month and year

    Browse your plans in four views, edit one occurrence or a whole series, and undo mistakes.

Your events stay with you

There is no account and no database. What you type is sent to the AI only to understand it. Your events live in your browser until you close the page, so download them before you leave.
