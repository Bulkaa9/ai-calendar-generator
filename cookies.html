Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

AI Calendar sets no cookies. It loads no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from our own site. It only keeps two small preferences in your browser.

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser, often used to recognise you or to track you across sites. Local storage is a similar space in your browser, which a site can use to remember settings. Unlike cookies, it is never sent to our server.

What we store

Name Type Purpose Duration
ai-calendar.sidebar-width Local storage, strictly necessary Remembers the sidebar width you chose in the calendar Until you clear this site's data
ai-calendar.notice-dismissed Local storage, strictly necessary Remembers that you closed the cookie notice, so it does not appear again Until you clear this site's data

Both items only record a choice you made, stay on your device, and are never used to identify or track you. That is why we do not ask for consent: the notice you see on the site is there to inform you.

What we do not use

  • No cookies of any kind, from us or from anyone else
  • No analytics or measurement tools
  • No advertising, social media buttons or tracking pixels
  • No externally hosted fonts or scripts

If we ever add something that needs your consent, we will ask before using it and update this page.

How to remove what is stored

You can delete both items at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, usually under "Privacy" or "Site settings". The calendar keeps working: the sidebar returns to its default width and the notice appears again.
