Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: there is no account and no database. What you type in Ask AI is sent to OpenAI to be turned into events, and your calendar stays in your browser. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

AI Calendar ("we", "us") is a free web service, based in Quebec, Canada, that turns plain language into calendar events. This policy explains what personal information the service uses, why, and the rights you have.

The person in charge of the protection of personal information for AI Calendar can be reached at [contact email].

2. What we collect and why

We only use the information needed to provide the service:

  • What you type in Ask AI, your time zone, and, only when you ask to change events, the title, dates, times, time zone and repeat rule of your existing events. We use them to turn your request into events.
  • Your IP address, which every website receives, to deliver the site and to limit how many AI requests one address can make, which protects the service from abuse.
  • Technical error logs, to find and fix problems.

Event locations, descriptions and reminders are never sent anywhere. The full list, with how long each item is kept, is on the Data we collect page.

We do not collect your name, email address or any account information, and we do not use the information for any other purpose, such as marketing or profiling.

3. Consent and legal basis

By using Ask AI, you consent to what you type being processed as described in this policy. You can withdraw that consent at any time by not using Ask AI: you can still create and export events by hand, and nothing is then sent to our server.

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we process your information because it is necessary to provide the service you request, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

4. Who receives your information

We use two service providers, both in the United States. Your information is therefore processed outside Quebec and Canada, where privacy laws may offer different protection.

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the website and our server. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI turns your text into events. OpenAI may keep requests for a limited time under its API data policies. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We do not store your text or your events. Your calendar lives in your browser until you close or reload the page. IP addresses are held in our server's memory only while it runs, and request and error logs are kept by Vercel for a limited time. Details are on the Data we collect page.

6. Automated processing

The AI only suggests events from your own words. You review every suggestion before it is added, and no decision about you is made by automated means.

7. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and our server keeps no database. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please do not enter sensitive information in Ask AI.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, Canada's PIPEDA, and the GDPR, you can:

  • ask whether we hold information about you, and get access to it
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • withdraw your consent
  • receive your information in a structured, commonly used format
  • file a complaint with a privacy authority

Because we keep no account or database, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. Your calendar data is on your own device, where you control it. To make a request, write to [contact email]. We answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Children

AI Calendar is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Cookies

We do not use cookies. We store two small preferences in your browser. See the Cookie Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, write to [contact email].
