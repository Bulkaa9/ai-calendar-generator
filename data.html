Data we collect

Last updated: October 28, 2026

AI Calendar collects only what it needs to turn your words into events. There is no account, no database, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. This page lists every piece of data the service uses.

Data needed to run the service

Data When Why it is necessary Who receives it How long it is kept
The text you type in Ask AI When you press Add events or Change events To understand your request and turn it into events Our server (hosted by Vercel) and OpenAI Not stored by us. OpenAI may keep it for a limited time under its API data policies.
Your time zone With each Ask AI request To place events at the right time Our server and OpenAI Not stored by us
Your existing events: title, dates, times, time zone and repeat rule Only when you press Change events So the AI knows which event you want to change Our server and OpenAI Not stored by us. Locations, descriptions and reminders are never sent.
Your IP address Whenever you load a page or use Ask AI To deliver the website, and to limit each address to 20 AI requests every 10 minutes, which protects the service from abuse Vercel, our hosting provider. Our server holds it in memory for the limit. In our server's memory only while that server instance runs, usually minutes to hours; never written to storage. Vercel's request logs: a limited time set by Vercel.
Error logs When a request fails To find and fix problems. If the AI returns an unreadable answer, that answer is logged, and it can contain event details. Vercel A limited time set by Vercel

Data that stays on your device

Data Why How long it is kept
Your calendar To show your events and create the .ics file you download. The file is made in your browser. Until you close or reload the page
Sidebar width (ai-calendar.sidebar-width) To remember the width you chose Until you clear this site's data in your browser
Notice closed (ai-calendar.notice-dismissed) So the cookie notice does not appear again Until you clear this site's data in your browser

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account of any kind
  • No cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools
  • No third-party scripts or fonts: everything on the page is served from our own site
  • We never sell or share your data for marketing

Please avoid sensitive information

Because what you type in Ask AI is sent to OpenAI, do not include sensitive information such as health details, passwords or identification numbers. If you need them, add them yourself in an event's location or description fields, which never leave your browser.
